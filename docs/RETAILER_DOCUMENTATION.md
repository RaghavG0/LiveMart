## Viewing Reviews

### Accessing Your Reviews

1. Log in to your LiveMart retailer dashboard
2. Navigate to **Reviews** in the left sidebar
3. Filter by:
   - Product
   - Rating (1-5 stars)
   - Date range
   - Review status (Pending reply, Replied, Flagged)

### Review Information

Each review shows:
- ⭐ **Rating**: 1-5 stars
- 📝 **Comment**: Customer feedback
- 📷 **Images**: Customer-uploaded photos (if any)
- ✓ **Verified Purchase**: Badge for confirmed orders
- 📅 **Date**: When the review was submitted
- 👤 **Customer**: Name (anonymized if requested)

### Review Status
- **Published**: Live on product page
- **Pending Moderation**: Under review by LiveMart team
- **Flagged**: Reported by users or auto-detected
- **Hidden**: Not visible to customers

## Responding to Reviews

### Why Respond?

Responding to reviews:
- ✅ Shows you care about customer feedback
- ✅ Improves trust and credibility
- ✅ Can turn negative experiences into positive ones
- ✅ Increases your response rate metric

### How to Respond

1. Click on any review
2. Click **Reply** button
3. Write your response (max 500 characters)
4. Click **Submit Reply**

Your reply will appear below the customer's review.

### Response Guidelines

**DO:**
- ✅ Respond within 24-48 hours
- ✅ Thank customers for positive reviews
- ✅ Acknowledge issues in negative reviews
- ✅ Offer solutions or contact info for support
- ✅ Be professional and courteous
- ✅ Personalize your response

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Be defensive or argumentative
- ❌ Share private customer information
- ❌ Ask customers to change/delete reviews
- ❌ Include promotional content or links
- ❌ Use generic copy-paste responses

## Best Practices

### For Positive Reviews (4-5 stars)

```
Example Response:

"Thank you so much for your kind words, Sarah! We're thrilled to hear you're enjoying the product. Your satisfaction is our top priority, and we look forward to serving you again soon!"
```

**Tips:**
- Express genuine gratitude
- Mention specific details they mentioned
- Keep it concise (50-100 words)
- Encourage repeat business

### For Neutral Reviews (3 stars)

```
Example Response:

"Hi Michael, thank you for sharing your feedback. We're glad you found the quality good, but sorry to hear about the delivery delay. We're actively working with our logistics partners to improve. If you have any other concerns, please reach out to support@livemart.com."
```

**Tips:**
- Acknowledge both positives and negatives
- Address specific concerns
- Provide solutions or next steps
- Invite further communication

### For Negative Reviews (1-2 stars)

```
Example Response:

"Hi Alex, we sincerely apologize for your experience. This is not the standard we hold ourselves to. We'd love to make this right. Please contact us at support@livemart.com or call 1-800-XXX-XXXX with your order number, and we'll resolve this immediately."
```

**Tips:**
- Apologize genuinely
- Don't make excuses
- Offer a clear path to resolution
- Take the conversation offline when possible
- Follow up after resolving

### Response Time Targets

| Priority | Target Response Time |
|----------|---------------------|
| 1-2 star reviews | Within 24 hours |
| 3 star reviews | Within 48 hours |
| 4-5 star reviews | Within 3-5 days |

## Analytics Dashboard

### Accessing Analytics

Navigate to **Dashboard → Analytics → Reviews** to see:

### Key Metrics

1. **Average Rating**
   - Overall rating across all products
   - Trend over time
   - Breakdown by product

2. **Total Reviews**
   - Total number of reviews
   - Growth rate
   - Reviews per product

3. **Response Rate**
   - Percentage of reviews you've responded to
   - Target: >80% for optimal performance

4. **NPS Score** (Net Promoter Score)
   - Measures customer loyalty
   - Calculation: (Promoters - Detractors) / Total × 100
   - Promoters: 4-5 stars
   - Detractors: 1-2 stars

5. **Review Velocity**
   - Reviews per week/month
   - Helps identify trending products

### Weekly Reports

You'll receive automated weekly email reports containing:
- Performance summary
- Top-rated products
- Products needing attention
- Response rate
- Recent trends

### Exporting Data

1. Go to Analytics Dashboard
2. Select date range
3. Click **Export** → Choose format (CSV/PDF)
4. Data includes: Rating, comment, date, product, response status

## Managing Product Ratings

### How Ratings Are Calculated

Product rating = Average of all approved review ratings

```
Example:
10 reviews: 5★, 5★, 4★, 4★, 4★, 3★, 3★, 2★, 2★, 1★
Average = (5+5+4+4+4+3+3+2+2+1) / 10 = 3.3★
```

### Improving Your Ratings

1. **Quality Control**
   - Ensure product matches description
   - Accurate images and specifications
   - Quality packaging

2. **Customer Service**
   - Fast shipping
   - Responsive support
   - Easy returns/exchanges

3. **Encourage Reviews**
   - Follow up after delivery
   - Make review process easy
   - Incentivize (within policy)

4. **Learn from Feedback**
   - Identify common complaints
   - Address recurring issues
   - Improve based on suggestions

### Rating Distribution

Healthy distribution typically looks like:
- 5 stars: 60-70%
- 4 stars: 20-25%
- 3 stars: 5-10%
- 1-2 stars: <5%

If you see unusual patterns (e.g., all 5-star or many 1-star), investigate for:
- Product quality issues
- Listing inaccuracies
- Fake reviews (report to LiveMart)

## FAQs

### Q: Can I delete negative reviews?

**A:** No. All legitimate reviews remain on the platform. However, you can:
- Report reviews that violate guidelines (spam, offensive, fake)
- Respond professionally to address concerns
- Work to improve and earn better reviews over time

### Q: How long does moderation take?

**A:** Most reviews are moderated within 24-48 hours. Reviews with images or flagged content may take longer.

### Q: Can I edit my reply?

**A:** Yes, within 24 hours of posting. After that, contact support@livemart.com.

### Q: Why was my reply rejected?

**A:** Replies may be rejected if they:
- Contain promotional content
- Include personal contact information
- Violate community guidelines
- Are off-topic or inappropriate

### Q: How do I report a fake review?

**A:**
1. Click on the review
2. Click **Report** → Select "Fake/Fraudulent"
3. Provide evidence if available
4. Our team will investigate within 48 hours

### Q: Can customers edit reviews after posting?

**A:** Customers can edit reviews within 7 days of posting. Edits are re-moderated.

### Q: How does LiveMart prevent fake reviews?

**A:**
- Verified purchase badges
- Anti-spam detection
- Duplicate content blocking
- User reputation scoring
- Manual moderation for flagged content

### Q: What if I disagree with a review?

**A:** You cannot delete or dispute reviews, but you can:
- Respond politely with your perspective
- Provide context or clarification
- Offer to resolve the issue privately

## Review Policy Summary

### Allowed Content
- Honest product experiences
- Factual descriptions
- Personal opinions
- Constructive criticism
- Product photos

### Prohibited Content
- Spam or promotional content
- Offensive language or hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Fake or incentivized reviews
- Competitor mentions
- Contact information (email, phone)
- Duplicate content

### Reporting Violations

If you see reviews violating these policies:
1. Click **Report** on the review
2. Select violation type
3. Provide details
4. Submit

Reports are reviewed within 48 hours.

## Tips for Success

### Maximize Your Review Performance

1. **Be Proactive**
   - Check reviews daily
   - Respond quickly to negative reviews
   - Thank customers for positive feedback

2. **Be Consistent**
   - Maintain your response rate above 80%
   - Use a consistent, professional tone
   - Have template responses ready (but personalize!)

3. **Be Data-Driven**
   - Review analytics weekly
   - Identify patterns in feedback
   - Adjust products/service based on insights

4. **Be Customer-Centric**
   - Put customers first in responses
   - Offer solutions, not excuses
   - Follow through on promises

### Success Metrics

Track these monthly:
- ✅ Response rate: >80%
- ✅ Average rating: >4.0
- ✅ NPS Score: >50
- ✅ Average response time: <48 hours
